## LinkedIn

A homeowner remodel looked like the best job on the list at 25% gross margin. After 140 hours of texts, revisions, and $7,500 of rework, the true margin was 7.8%.

That's one of three sample customers in our latest post for contractors. The general contractor was the biggest account at $310,000 in revenue, but after office time, back-charges, and the cost of waiting to get paid, it earned just 6.1%. The repeat developer, on only $240,000, came in at 18.3%.

Job-cost reports rarely show those hidden costs. A customer-level view does, and it makes the next step clearer: set a minimum margin, try a price increase or a 25 to 30 percent deposit first, and only then decide who to stop bidding for. Winter is a good time to do it, before spring crews and materials are committed.
