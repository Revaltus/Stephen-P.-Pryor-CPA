## LinkedIn

Say you rent a skid steer for $1,100 a month on a 12-month agreement that renews automatically unless you give 60 days' written notice. The work dries up in October, the notice date slips by, and you owe $13,200 for a machine sitting in the yard.

A business compliance calendar catches exactly this kind of quiet cost. The date that matters is the notice deadline, not the end of the term.

Our December post shows Port Chester contractors how to put every insurance renewal, license expiration, contract notice, payroll filing, and state report on one page in an afternoon. Insurance goes first, with renewal quotes requested from your broker 60 to 90 days out instead of 10. Then come the first-quarter dates, including W-2s, 1099-NECs, and fourth-quarter payroll returns due January 31.

Our Port Chester CPA team sees these surprises every winter. The slow stretch after Christmas is the best time to fix them.
