## Bookkeeping support built for busy business owners

Running a business in Port Chester means your attention is already split between customers, crews, staff, and suppliers. Bookkeeping shouldn't be one more thing competing for it. Stephen P. Pryor, CPA handles the daily work of transaction classification, expense tracking, and invoicing so your books stay current without you touching a spreadsheet.

That's the idea behind our tagline: you run your business, we run your numbers. It's not a slogan we use loosely. When you hand off bookkeeping to our team, you get someone who actually looks at what's happening in your accounts each month, catches errors before they become problems, and flags anything that needs your attention. You stay informed without staying buried in the details.

For owners who've been doing their own books at night, or paying a part-time bookkeeper who disappears at tax time, this is a different kind of arrangement. One where the numbers are handled, and you know it.

## What our monthly bookkeeping includes

Every client gets the same disciplined process, whether you're running a five-person contracting crew or a neighborhood bar. Each month, we reconcile your bank and credit card accounts, categorize every transaction, and keep your books ready before you ever need them to be.

- Bank account reconciliation
- Electronic transaction reconciliation into QuickBooks
- Credit card account reconciliation
- Sales tax preparation and filing
- Transaction categorization and coding
- Invoicing and accounts receivable tracking
- Monthly financial reporting and statements
- General ledger maintenance

Bookkeeping isn't sold on its own here. It's bundled with tax and payroll compliance for one fixed monthly fee, so there's no surprise invoice for a phone call or a question about a transaction. Firms that bill by the hour tend to make clients think twice before reaching out. We'd rather you call.

## Real-time financial visibility through our secure platform

A lot of owners tell us the same thing before they become clients: they don't know where their money stands until someone tells them, and by then it's old news. Our secure online platform fixes that. You get real-time access to your financial data, reports, and documents whenever you need them, from wherever you are.

No more waiting for a quarterly meeting to find out if last month was actually profitable. No more digging through old email threads for a document you sent six months ago. Everything lives in one place, updated as we work, protected behind secure login credentials. If you want to check your cash position before signing a lease or hiring a fourth employee, you can, in the moment it matters.

## Bookkeeping tailored to construction, real estate, and bars and restaurants

General bookkeeping software can track expenses. It can't tell you which of your projects hemorrhaged the most in overtime last quarter, or whether your tip reporting would hold up under review. That takes bookkeeping built around how these industries actually make and lose money.

### Construction
icon: Hammer

Job costing is where most contractor books fall apart. We track labor, materials, and subcontractor costs by project so you know which jobs are actually profitable, not just whether the business overall is in the black. That visibility also keeps cash flow manageable between draw schedules and payment phases.

### Real Estate
icon: Building2

Whether you hold a handful of rental units or run a growing portfolio across multiple entities, your books need to reflect ownership structure accurately. We track income and expenses at the entity level and keep records organized for depreciation schedules and cost segregation opportunities your tax return can actually use.

### Bars and Restaurants
icon: Wallet

Thin margins mean every dollar has to be accounted for, from daily cash drawer counts to tip reporting to the vendor invoices that arrive every week. We reconcile sales, cash, and card deposits on the schedule your operation needs, so you always know what's landing in the bank versus what's just on paper.

## How we onboard your business

Switching bookkeepers, or handing off the job for the first time, feels risky. Our onboarding process is built so it doesn't feel that way.

### We start with a conversation
We ask how your business runs day to day: your existing systems, your software, your pain points, and what "on top of it" actually looks like for you.

### We review your current setup
If you're already using QuickBooks or another platform, we look at what's there, clean up what needs cleaning, and map out what stays and what changes.

### We build your books
We set up or migrate your accounts, establish your monthly reconciliation schedule, and connect you to our secure client platform.

### We keep talking
Onboarding isn't a one-time event. We check in as your business changes, because the way you operated at launch usually isn't how you'll operate at year three.
