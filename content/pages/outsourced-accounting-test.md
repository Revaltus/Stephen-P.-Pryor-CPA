## Outsourced accounting services for contractors, landlords, and bar owners

Stop losing margin on jobs you can't track. Maybe it's a project that ran over on labor, a rental property with muddy books, or a bar whose sales tax return never quite matches the register. The problem is usually the same: nobody has time to keep the numbers clean, and nobody is checking them.

Stephen P. Pryor, CPA, runs an outsourced accounting practice out of Port Chester, NY, built for exactly that. Bookkeeping, tax, and payroll compliance come together for one fixed monthly fee. A CPA-led team handles the work, so you skip the full-time hire you would have to find, train, and pay benefits to.

It fits construction companies, real estate owners and property managers, restaurants, bars and pubs, service businesses, and new owners setting up for the first time. If you are searching for a small business accountant in Port Chester, or anywhere across New York, New Jersey, and Connecticut, this is the setup that takes the work off your desk.

Before any work begins, we listen. How do you invoice? Who do you pay, and when? What does your crew or staff schedule look like, and what keeps you up at night? The accounting gets built around those answers, not the other way around.

You run your business. We'll run your numbers.

## What your monthly fee covers

One fixed fee covers the recurring work, so a quarter-end or a filing deadline never arrives with a surprise invoice attached.

- Bank account reconciliation every month
- Credit card account reconciliation
- Electronic transaction reconciliation into QuickBooks
- Sales tax preparation and filing
- Payroll tax filings, plus W-2 and 1099 processing
- Pension and garnishment remittance
- Federal and state tax preparation with e-filing, plus an annual tax plan
- Estimated payment reminders
- General business consulting and regular check-ins on your numbers
- Secure online platform access to your financial data
- Year-round support from people who already know your file
- Certified payroll and prevailing wage compliance support for contractors on public works contracts

Day to day, the work looks different depending on your business. Contractors get job-cost tracking so you can see profit by project. Landlords and property managers get books organized by property, with trust accounting where it applies. Bars and restaurants get tip reporting and sales tax handled with cash-heavy sales in mind.
