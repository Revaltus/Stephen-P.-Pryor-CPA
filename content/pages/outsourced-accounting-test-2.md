## Outsourced Accounting

You run your business. We'll run your numbers. Our outsourced accounting service gives you a full accounting department for one fixed monthly fee, with bookkeeping, payroll, and business tax handled together by people who know your industry and your name.

## Stop Losing Margin on Jobs, Tabs, and Properties You Can't Track

Most owners we meet are not short on effort. They are short on clear numbers. A contractor can't tell which job made money. A bar owner sees sales climbing but can't see where the cash goes. A landlord wonders whether the depreciation is being used well.

Our outsourced accounting service covers account reconciliation, tax planning and preparation, payroll compliance, sales tax, pension and garnishment remittance, 1099 preparation, and general business consulting. You get one team, one monthly fee, and a secure online platform where you can see your financial data any time.

From our office in Port Chester, we serve construction companies, real estate businesses, restaurants, bars and pubs, and service-based businesses across New York, New Jersey, and Connecticut. When you join us, we take the time to learn your internal systems first, so the work fits how you already operate.

## Bookkeeping: Clean Books Every Month

Good bookkeeping is the foundation for everything else. When your records are accurate and current, tax time is calmer, payroll is easier, and decisions stop feeling like guesses.

Each month we reconcile your bank accounts, credit card accounts, and electronic transactions into QuickBooks. That discipline matters. It catches duplicate charges, missed deposits, and miscoded expenses while they are small, instead of months later when they are hard to untangle. We also prepare and file your sales tax, so one more deadline comes off your plate.

The way we set up your books depends on your industry:

- **Construction:** We track job costs so you can see profitability on each project, not just in total. That helps with the uneven cash flow between project phases.
- **Real estate and property management:** We keep property-level records and handle trust accounting where it applies, so owners, tenants, and brokers can each see what they need.
- **Restaurants, bars, and pubs:** We organize sales, cost of goods, tips, and card fees so you can see where thin margins are going.

If your books need a better foundation, we can also advise on the right accounting platform and design a chart of accounts around how your business really works. Because you have secure online access, you never have to wait for us to find out where you stand.

## Payroll: Filed Correctly in Every State You Work In

Payroll looks simple until a quarter-end filing goes wrong. Rates differ between New York, New Jersey, and Connecticut. Seasonal staff come and go. Tipped employees add reporting rules. We handle the payroll tax filings and compliance for you, along with W-2 and 1099 processing, so deadlines are met and notices don't show up in your mailbox.

For restaurants, bars, and pubs, that means support for multi-location and seasonal staffing and for tip reporting. For growing small businesses, it means a reliable process that doesn't depend on one person in your office remembering a due date.

Contractors get extra attention. If you work on public works contracts, we provide certified payroll and prevailing wage compliance support, so the reports are accurate and complete. We can also take care of pension and garnishment remittances, which are easy to miss and costly when late.

Because payroll flows directly into your bookkeeping and tax work, the numbers match across every report. Nothing is entered twice, and nothing falls between the cracks.

## Business Tax: Planned All Year, Not Just in April

Tax is much easier when the same team keeps your books and runs your payroll. We already know your numbers, so your return reflects the whole year, and there are fewer surprises at filing time.

We prepare your federal and state business returns with e-filing, and you can share and receive records through our secure online platform. We build an annual tax plan, send estimated payment reminders, and check in during the year so you can make decisions before year-end, when there is still time to act.

What that looks like in practice:

- **Business owners:** Entity structure review and tax planning, so you aren't paying more than you should for how your business is set up.
- **Contractors:** Planning around project timing, job profitability, and 1099 requirements.
- **Real estate owners and investors:** Guidance on depreciation, passive activity rules, and 1031 exchanges.
- **Restaurants, bars, and pubs:** Help with sales tax and with cash handling and tip reporting that keep you compliant.
- **Owner-operated corporations:** Personal and corporate returns prepared together, so the two always agree.

If you are launching something new, we can also help you choose the right entity type, set up your accounting system, and compare retirement plan options from the start.
