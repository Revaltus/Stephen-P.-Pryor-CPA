## Meet the team

### Stephen P. Pryor, CPA
Founder

Stephen founded the firm on the belief that accounting works best when it's personal. He works directly with construction, real estate, and hospitality clients across the tri-state area, building the kind of long-term relationships where clients call before a problem becomes a crisis, not after. [Read more about Stephen.](/who-we-are/stephen-pryor)

### Shawn Wilson, CPA
Managing Director

Shawn brings deep tax and advisory experience to construction and real estate clients, translating complex entity and depreciation questions into decisions owners can actually act on. [Read more about Shawn.](/who-we-are/shawn-wilson)

### Nicholas Vergara
photo: nicholas-vergara_nizgdf.jpg
Senior Manager

Nicholas oversees bookkeeping and payroll compliance engagements, keeping the daily numbers accurate so tax time never turns into a scramble. [Read more about Nicholas.](/who-we-are/nicholas-vergara)

### Denise Romano, CPA
Senior Accountant

Denise works closely with individuals and small business owners on personal and business tax preparation, catching deductions that get missed when a return is rushed. [Read more about Denise.](/who-we-are/denise-romano)

Behind Stephen, Shawn, Nicholas, and Denise is an office team handling billing, onboarding, and day-to-day coordination, the people who make sure nothing falls through the cracks between meetings.
